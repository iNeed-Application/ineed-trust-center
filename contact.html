Account security

If you believe your account was accessed without permission, first secure your email and connected Google or Facebook account. Then contact us using the subject Urgent iNeed Account Security.

Do not email passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, app secrets or private keys.

What to include

  • The email associated with your iNeed account.
  • The authentication method involved: password, Google or Facebook.
  • A concise description of the request.
  • Any relevant date or safe reference number.
Protect your identityDo not attach government IDs, fiscal documents or sensitive credentials unless iNeed specifically requests an appropriate secure verification method.
