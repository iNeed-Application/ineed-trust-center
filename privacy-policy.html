This policy explains how iNeed collects, uses, protects and deletes personal information when you use the application or authenticate with an external provider.

1. Scope and responsible party

This Privacy Policy applies to the iNeed web application, its account services, vendor experiences and related development environments made available to authorized users. The service is operated from Mexico by the iNeed Application development team (“iNeed”, “we”, “us”).

Development statusiNeed is currently under active development. Features and processing activities may evolve. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date.

2. Information we process

Account and profile information

  • Username, name, email address, optional backup email and phone number.
  • Gender selection, country code and optional birth date.
  • Account roles, verification status, creation date and security settings.
  • Passwords stored only as one-way cryptographic hashes. We do not store plaintext passwords.

Social authentication information

  • The authentication provider, such as Google or Facebook.
  • The provider-specific user identifier, email claim and email-verification status.
  • We do not receive or store your Google or Facebook password.

Security and technical information

  • Login time, success or failure status, IP address and browser user-agent information.
  • Account lock events, password update history and security notification events.
  • Short-lived verification, password-recovery and onboarding records.

Vendor and catalog information

  • Business profile, business contact details and vendor status.
  • Fiscal identity information and documents submitted for vendor verification.
  • Product information, categories, media, variants and pricing information.

3. How we use information

We process information to:

  • Create, verify, secure and administer accounts.
  • Authenticate users through passwords or a chosen social sign-in provider.
  • Prevent fraud, investigate suspicious activity and enforce access controls.
  • Provide user, vendor, product and account-management functionality.
  • Send transactional messages such as verification, recovery, password-change and security notifications.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
  • Diagnose failures and improve the reliability and usability of the service.

We do not sell personal information and do not use social sign-in information for third-party advertising.

4. Google and Facebook sign-in

When you choose a social sign-in method, the provider authenticates you and returns limited identity claims authorized by you. iNeed uses the stable provider identifier as the ownership key. An email address alone is not sufficient to silently link an external identity to an existing account.

You can review the provider’s own privacy practices through Google or Meta. Disconnecting iNeed in the provider’s settings stops future authorization but does not automatically delete information already held by iNeed. Follow our data deletion instructions to request deletion.

5. When information may be shared

We disclose information only as reasonably necessary:

  • To infrastructure, email, database, hosting or security providers acting on our instructions.
  • To Google, Meta or another provider when you initiate or manage the applicable social sign-in flow.
  • To authorized iNeed reviewers when a vendor submits verification information.
  • To comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse or defend legal rights.
  • As part of a future organizational transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice obligations.

Service providers should receive only the information required for their function and remain subject to contractual or legal safeguards.

6. Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while the account remains active and for a limited period afterward when necessary for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance or backup integrity. Expired authentication tokens are retained only as needed for security auditing and cleanup. Vendor records may require longer retention when legal or fraud-prevention obligations apply.

When a valid deletion request is completed, personal information is deleted or irreversibly de-identified unless retention is required by law or necessary to protect legitimate security interests. Backup copies may remain until their scheduled rotation.

7. Security

iNeed uses role-based authorization, one-way password hashing, single-use recovery tokens, account-lock protections, session controls, database constraints and security event logging. Access to vendor verification documents is restricted to their owner and authorized reviewers.

No system can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or information is at risk, contact us promptly and secure any connected identity-provider account.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and appropriate identity verification, you may request access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or portability of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis, without affecting prior lawful processing.

You can update certain account details from your workspace. For deletion, follow the account and data deletion process.

Children

iNeed is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly request that minors create vendor accounts or submit fiscal documentation. If you believe a minor provided information, contact us for review.

International access

Authentication and infrastructure providers may process information outside Mexico. Where applicable, we rely on provider commitments and safeguards appropriate to the relevant transfer.

9. Contact and policy changes

Questions, rights requests and privacy concerns may be sent to bryanfranco_94@hotmail.com. Include “iNeed Privacy Request” in the subject and do not send passwords, identity-provider secrets or unnecessary documents.

We may update this policy when the service, law or processing practices change. The current version and effective date will always appear on this page.
