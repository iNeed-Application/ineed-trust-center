1. Request deletion from iNeed

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your iNeed account to bryanfranco_94@hotmail.com.
  2. Use the subject iNeed Data Deletion Request and identify whether you signed in with a password, Google or Facebook.
  3. Do not include your password, access token, app secret, government identifiers or fiscal documents in the email.
  4. Complete the reasonable identity-verification step we provide. This prevents unauthorized deletion requests.
  5. After verification, we will confirm receipt and provide the request status or completion notice.
Deleting provider access is not the same as deleting your iNeed account.Removing iNeed from Facebook or Google stops future provider authorization. You must still submit the request above if you want information stored by iNeed deleted.

2. Remove iNeed from Facebook

You can revoke iNeed’s access from Facebook independently of the iNeed deletion request:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy, then Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites.
  3. Select iNeed and choose Remove.
  4. Then submit the iNeed deletion request above to delete the associated internal account and external identity record.

Facebook’s labels may change over time. Refer to Meta’s current account settings if the navigation differs.

3. What the request covers

Subject to the exceptions below, a verified deletion request covers:

  • The iNeed user account and profile information.
  • The external identity association for Google, Facebook or another connected provider.
  • Provider subject identifiers and provider email metadata held by iNeed.
  • Password-recovery and email-verification records associated with the account.
  • Vendor profile, verification and catalog information owned solely by the requesting account, where deletion is legally and technically permitted.

Information may instead be irreversibly de-identified when deletion would undermine aggregate integrity or where anonymization better protects other users.

4. Response timeline

We aim to acknowledge verified requests promptly and complete them within 30 calendar days. Complex requests may require additional time where permitted by law. If an extension is necessary, we will explain the reason and expected completion date.

After completion, residual encrypted backups may retain limited information until their normal rotation. Those backups are not used for ordinary product operations.

5. Limited retention exceptions

We may retain narrowly scoped information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with tax, accounting, regulatory or legal obligations.
  • Detect security incidents, fraud, abuse or unauthorized access.
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements.
  • Protect the rights and safety of users or third parties.
  • Maintain suppression records needed to honor the deletion request.
